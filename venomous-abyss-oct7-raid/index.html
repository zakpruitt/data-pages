What to work on next week

  1. Make the second Burst routine. It ended 11 pulls, more than anything else. On the pulls that made it through (12, 20, 21, 26, 30), the raid was mostly alive going in. Compare a 1:48 wipe like pull 16 with pull 26 to see what changed in healing cooldowns and positions.
  2. Plan Ravenous Feast with players lost. It ended pulls 25, 28, 31 and 32 at about 2:00 to 2:06. By then, pulls 28 and 31 had already lost five and six players, most of them at the second Burst, so the soak was short on people. The soak plan needs a backup for each soaker.
  3. Stop the Caustic Globule chains at 1:22. Globules killed 115 players and ended pulls 5, 11, 13 and 19. Method's guide says players drop globules when they die at max Eternal Venom stacks, and any crowd control removes their Barbed Bulwark shield. Assign AoE stuns to that moment.
  4. Get ready for Stone Breaker at 3:00 to 4:00. Mass Stone Breaker deaths ended both of the best pulls (12 and 26 at 4:00) and pull 21 at 3:02. The Warcraft Wiki says Stone Breaker shakes the whole platform if it hits no player, so check who is meant to take it.
  5. Survive the opener. Pulls 1 and 27 died within 30 seconds after the tank died to Concentrated Spittle at 0:05 or 0:06. The tank should use a defensive for the first Spittle.
  6. Cut the early deaths. Anchorhowl had 8, the most in the raid, and died first in 4 pulls; losing a healer early hurts every pull. Kirillas and Aarragorn had 7 each and Vilkas had 6.

What to aim for

  1. Plan Obsidian Scales around the Bursts. Visceral Burst killed Bigchomper 7 times. Scales was active for the second Burst on only 4 of the 18 pulls where Bigchomper was alive for it, and on 8 pulls it was never used. Scales has two charges and the Bursts are about 60 seconds apart, so use one charge at about 0:43 and the other at about 1:43. The first charge comes back in time for the third Burst at about 3:20.
  2. Use the rest of your defensives too. Healthstone was used once in 22 pulls, and Renewing Blaze never appears in the log. A Healthstone after the first Burst, plus Renewing Blaze for the second if you have it talented, makes those deaths less likely. Zephyr was used on 5 pulls; telling the raid when you'll press it helps the healers plan.
  3. Move out of Toxic Fumes sooner. Bigchomper took 31k Toxic Fumes damage per second, the most in the raid (the raid median was about 27k), and it was the first death on pull 32. If ranged positioning keeps you in it to keep casting, Hover lets you move while casting.
  4. Know your Ravenous Feast set. Feast killed Bigchomper 5 times, including the first death of pull 22 at 1:04. On Heroic, Mythic Trap says the soak leaves a damage-taken debuff and each player can soak only once per set; check whether Mythic works the same way. Confirm which set you're in before stepping in.
  5. Keep the damage where it is. Bigchomper was top DPS on 17 of 21 pulls and parsed 90 or higher on all of them. At this point, staying alive adds more to a pull than extra damage does.
