Raid night, October 1

Sszorak went down on the 11th pull. The rest of the night went to The Twin Fangs: 23 pulls, the best one ending at 63.2%. Nearly every Twin Fangs wipe came at the same two moments, both of them Visceral Burst.

The short version

  • Sszorak was a clean kill. The kill pull had only 3 deaths. Wipes before it came from Venomous Surge wiping the raid in one second (pulls 7 and 9), Virulence at 3:19 (pull 10), and Mutilate or Mutilated Gash deaths late in the longer pulls.
  • Twin Fangs has two walls, and both are Visceral Burst. 11 pulls ended at about 0:45 with the raid dying to the first Burst, and 7 more ended at about 1:48 to the second. Only one pull got past both.
  • The first wall got solved mid-night. In the first 11 pulls, the raid got past the first Burst once. In the last 12, it got past it 9 times.
  • Caustic Globule is the next problem. It caused 69 deaths, the second most, and it picked people off before the Burst in 6 different pulls. The best pull ended to Vile Flood and then a Globule wipe at 2:42, the only time the raid saw that part of the fight.

What to work on next week

  1. Lock in the first Visceral Burst. The raid cleared it 9 of the last 12 pulls but lost pulls 17 and 21 to it again. Whatever changed around pull 12 should be written down and called the same way every pull.
  2. Plan the second Burst at 1:48. Seven of the last 12 pulls died there with most of the raid alive. It is now the main wall between the raid and new parts of the fight.
  3. Stop losing people to Caustic Globule before the Burst. It killed players early in pulls 1, 3, 12, 13, 16 and 18, and it ended pulls 2, 12 and 19 outright.
  4. Learn Vile Flood. The raid only saw it once, on the 63% pull, where it killed six players between 2:20 and 2:33. It will matter as soon as the second Burst is handled.
  5. Cut the early deaths. Vilkas died before the wipe 7 times on Twin Fangs and also died on the Sszorak kill. Smolp, Bigchomper and Anchorhowl each had 4 early deaths.
