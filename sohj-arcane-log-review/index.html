Sohj Arcane Log Review

Sohj's +17 Den of Nalorakk next to Azunazx's +22 Den of Nalorakk, checked cast by cast against the Wowhead Arcane rotation guide. Same dungeon, near-identical talents, the same trinkets, almost the same clear time, and 56% more DPS on Azunazx's side. This page splits that gap into the part the key level explains and the part Sohj can fix.

Sohj's log Azunazx's log Wowhead rotation guide

The short version

Most of the gap is the key level. Azunazx's group had to deal 1.62 times as much damage to clear the same dungeon in the same half hour, and each mage did 28% of their group's damage. Against other Arcane Mages in +17s, Sohj parsed 44. That parse is the real room to grow, and it comes from a handful of habits.

  1. 1Bank Arcane Salvo before Barraging, especially going into Arcane Surge. Early Barrages cost Prismatic Bolts, and that is where the extra Arcane Blasts come from.≈ 2–3% DPS
  2. 2Press Touch of the Magi every 45 seconds instead of saving it for Surge. 27 casts where about 38 were possible.≈ 1.5% DPS
  3. 3Get the first spell out sooner on each pull. About 30 seconds lost across the run.≈ 1–2% DPS
  4. 4Small leaks: Blasts with a proc up, Missiles at 25 Salvo, Arcane Explosions that hit nothing.≈ 1% DPS
  5. +Gear: 311 rings and a 318 neck are the cheapest upgrades toward Azunazx's 331.≈ 3–5% DPS

1
Biggest fix · ≈ 2–3% DPS

Bank Salvo before you Barrage

In 12.1 Sunfury, Arcane Barrage spends your Arcane Salvo, and the more it spends, the better its chance to proc Prismatic Bolt. In these two logs that chance was about 8% under 12 Salvo, 35% at 12–24, and 53% at the 25-stack cap. Every Prismatic Bolt cast also hands you a Clearcasting, which feeds Arcane Missiles, which builds Salvo for the next Barrage. Prismatic Bolt alone is 18–21% of both mages' damage.

Barrage at low Salvo→fewer Prismatic Bolts→fewer Clearcasts→nothing to press but Arcane Blast

That chain is the "too much Arcane Blast" in Sohj's log. He cast 5.3 Blasts per combat minute to Azunazx's 2.2, and 46 of them at 0–2 Arcane Charges (Azunazx: 6), almost all mid-pull. Blast is the symptom. The early Barrage upstream is the cause.

The Surge opener is the worst spot. Sohj usually presses Surge and Barrages right away, with whatever Salvo he has. What to do

  • Holding Clearcasting with under 12 Salvo: cast Arcane Missiles, not Barrage.
  • Outside Arcane Soul, Barrage only with Clearcasting and 12+ Salvo, or at 25 Salvo. Watch the Salvo count on the buff bar the way you watch Arcane Charges.
  • Going into Surge, have Salvo built. If it isn't, follow the guide's opener: Surge, one Arcane Missiles, Barrage, then Touch of the Magi while the Barrage is in the air.
  • At 0 charges with no procs, Arcane Orb comes before Arcane Blast.
  • Don't Missiles at 25 Salvo. Barrage first, then Missiles.

Touch of the Magi every 45 seconds

Touch of the Magi has a 45-second cooldown and Arcane Surge 90, so every other Touch lines up with Surge on its own. The guide says not to hold Touch more than a few seconds, especially the ones that aren't paired with Surge. Sohj tends to wait for Surge instead, and a skipped Touch costs more than its explosion: each cast also gives 4 Arcane Charges and a Clearcasting.

The Hoardmonger is the clearest example. Surge and Touch both came off cooldown about 40 seconds before the boss died, and both were saved for the next pack. Azunazx fit 3 Surges and 6 Touches into that boss; Sohj fit 2 and 4. On Spirit of Hunger and the pack after the first boss, Touch sat ready for 30–45 seconds waiting for Surge.

What to do

  • Press Touch whenever it's ready and the target will live about 12 more seconds. Most of its damage lands when it expires.
  • Surge every 90 seconds. Every second Touch will land in the Surge window without any planning.
  • Holding Surge at the end of a boss for the next pack is a fair call when the boss is nearly dead. 40 seconds is too long.

≈ 1–2% DPS

First spell out sooner

Seconds from the pull starting to each mage's first damaging spell. Over the 11 pulls both groups made, Sohj's first cast landed about 30 seconds later in total, or about 2 seconds per pull after the first. Some of that is how each tank pulls, but Azunazx is usually casting within a second or two.

What to do

  • Pre-cast or open with an instant the moment the tank has the pack. Touch of the Magi and Arcane Orb both hand out Arcane Charges, so neither wastes the opening.
  • Azunazx opens big trash pulls with Arcane Explosion while the mobs are stacked on the tank (42 casts, versus Sohj's 9, of which 7 hit nothing). It isn't in the guide's priority list, but it gets charges and damage going while the pack is gathering.

Small leaks

  • 21 Arcane Blasts cast while a better spell had been up for over half a second: Clearcasting was up for 15 of them and Salvo was capped at 25 for 9 (some had both). Azunazx: 3.
  • 15 Arcane Missiles at 25 Salvo. The Salvo those channels generate is thrown away. Azunazx: 9.
  • 7 Arcane Explosions that hit nothing out of 9 cast. Each is a wasted global cooldown.
  • 3 cancelled Arcane Surge casts. Surge has a cast time now, so start it where you won't need to move. Azunazx: 1.
  • 10 Prismatic Bolts at 0 Cumulative Power instead of 8. The tier set's stacks make the Bolt hit much harder. Azunazx: 6.
  • 17 Barrages under 4 Arcane Charges, 10 of them outside Arcane Soul. Azunazx: 2.

Upgrade order

Both rings (311) and the neck (318) are Sohj's lowest items and the cheapest to replace. After those, the 321 shoulders, chest, bracers and cloak.

Stats are fine

The guide's Sunfury priority is Intellect, then Haste, Versatility, Critical Strike, Mastery. Haste matters because more casts means more Prismatic Bolt chances. Sohj carries the most Haste of the two; he just has less of everything else because of item level.

Already right

  • Talents. Sohj's build matches Wowhead's Mythic+ Sunfury build except for one class node: Captured Thoughts where the guide takes Spellsteal. Azunazx is also one node off the guide (Mana Bomb).
  • Casting pace. 39 spells per combat minute, same as Azunazx.
  • Uptime inside pulls, about 94% to Azunazx's 96%. WCL's 81% vs 88% "active time" is lower mainly because the +17 group spent more of the run walking between pulls.
  • Surge setup. Freightrunner's Flask, potions and Time Warp went out with Surge almost every time. Surge usage overall (16 of about 20 possible) is close to Azunazx's (18 of 21).
  • Arcane Soul. Barrage spam during Soul and no Blasts. The 19 Prismatic Bolt procs overwritten during Soul are expected under the guide's priority, and Azunazx loses exactly the same 19.
  • Survival. No deaths, Prismatic Barrier kept rolling (the guide calls it a must-cast).

How this was measured

  • Every combat event for both players was pulled from Warcraft Logs (52,000 for Sohj, 61,800 for Azunazx) and replayed to rebuild Arcane Charges, Clearcasting, Arcane Salvo, Prismatic Bolt and Cumulative Power at the moment of each cast.
  • "In combat" means inside Warcraft Logs' pull windows. Per-minute rates use combat minutes (25.7 for Sohj, 28.1 for Azunazx).
  • "Possible" Touch and Surge casts assume pressing them the instant they come off cooldown during a pull, so they are an upper bound.
  • DPS gains are rough estimates priced with Sohj's own damage per cast. They overlap a little, so they won't add up exactly.
  • Luck: at 25 Salvo, Azunazx's Barrages procced Prismatic Bolt 59% of the time and Sohj's 47%. At these sample sizes that is most likely luck, and it is worth about 2–3% to Azunazx on its own.
